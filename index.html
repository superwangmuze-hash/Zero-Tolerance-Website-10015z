Zero Tolerance Robotics
Our team

WHO
WE ARE

Zero Tolerance is a VEX Robotics team where students explore engineering through hands-on projects. We work together to turn ideas into designs, build and program robots, and learn from each challenge.

Robotics brings creativity and problem-solving together. Along the way, we share ideas, test our work, and keep improving as a team.
